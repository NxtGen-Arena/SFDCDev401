# Roll up Contacts on Account
  
**Business Challenge**:
  
Business currently have no way to understand how many active Contacts are associated with the Account. Also, Accounts with Open opportunities are being marked as Inactive causing Forecasting issues. Also, business wants to track the largest Opportunity associated with each of its Accounts. This information could be used to prioritize sales efforts, allocate resources, or identify opportunities for cross-selling or upselling.

**What is Expected**: 
 
* Display count of Active contacts on Account level.
* Rollup final count of the contacts to the parent account (topmost account) associated.
* Active Contacts should only be considered. Assume all the possible scenarios.
* If the Account with Open Opportunities is being marked as Inactive, error should be displayed to the Reps.
* Largest Opportunity should be considered for - Closed-WOn and Closed -Lost status separately. Display the opportunity as a lookup on Account.
