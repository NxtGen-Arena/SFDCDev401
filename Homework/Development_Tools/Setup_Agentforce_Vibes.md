# Explore Agentforce Vibes

<h2>Sign Up for a Developer Edition Org</h2>
To complete hands-on projects, you need a special Developer Edition org with Agentforce Vibes:

1. Go to the Trailhead signup page for [Agentforce Vibes Developer Edition](https://trailhead.salesforce.com/promo/orgs/learn-agentforce-for-developers-on-trailhead).
2. Fill out the form with your email and a unique username (it looks like an email, but doesn’t need to be real).
3. Click Sign me up.
4. Check your email and click Verify Account.Set your password and challenge question. Keep these safe for later.
